Senior PM Playbook · AI Commerce

From Generative Assistants to Agentic Checkout

A fast, evidence-backed course for senior Product Managers on how AI is reshaping retail: the five waves of AI, how shopping behaviors are shifting, and how to make products and operations legible to autonomous agents.

Course Outcomes

  • Explain the five waves of AI in commerce and what “agentic checkout” changes for funnels and attribution.
  • Translate changing shopper behaviors into product requirements (universal cart, policy-aware bundles, review synthesis).
  • Redesign merchant JTBD toward Assistant/Agent Optimization (AIO), agent-ready data, and ACP/Instant Checkout.
  • Build a 12‑month roadmap with experiments, data standards, and risk controls.

Audience & Format

  • Audience: Senior PMs/Heads of Product in ecommerce, marketplaces, and retail platforms.
  • Format: 5 modules, each with briefs, decision frameworks, and PM action checklists.
  • Sources: Amazon Rufus, Google Shopping (Gemini + Shopping Graph), Instacart, Klarna, Walmart Sparky, OpenAI Instant Checkout/ACP.

Module 1 · The Five Waves of AI in Ecommerce & Retail

Wave 1 — Rules & heuristics (≈1999–2012)

Faceted search, basic collaborative filtering, coupon engines, and retargeting. Human rules drove “If user saw X, show Y.”

Wave 2 — Predictive ML at scale (≈2012–2022)

Deep-learning rankers for search/recs, dynamic pricing, demand forecasting, visual search, fulfillment optimization. Large catalogs + clickstream signals improved personalization and logistics.

Wave 3 — GenAI overlays (2023–2024)

LLM layers on catalogs: conversational discovery, AI review highlights, product copy generation, concierge-style support. Examples: Amazon Rufus, Google Shopping AI briefs, Instacart “Ask Instacart,” Klarna’s assistant handling ~700 agents worth of work.

Wave 4 — Agentic, multi-merchant checkout (2025→)

Assistants transact in-line. OpenAI’s Instant Checkout via ACP (Etsy live, Shopify coming) and Walmart’s Sparky signal a shift from search to plan/compare/buy inside chat.

Wave 5 — Ambient, autonomous commerce (2026–2030)

Multimodal agents plan, negotiate, check inventory/CO₂/ETA, orchestrate baskets across retailers under user policies. Standards like ACP + GS1 Digital Link + schema.org Product make product data trustworthy for agents.

Positioning statement: We are living the GenAI overlay (Wave 3) while building for agentic checkout (Wave 4) and ambient autonomy (Wave 5).

Module 2 · How Shopping Behaviors Are Changing

From click-and-compare → describe-and-delegate

Shoppers now specify goals (budget, dietary constraints, delivery window) and expect a shoppable plan with one-flow checkout—often across merchants—without leaving the assistant.

  • Goal shopping: “Furnish a studio for <$900; stage deliveries.”
  • Occasion planning: “Backyard dinner for 8, gluten-free, <$120” with recipe + tool bundles.
  • Show-to-shop: Snap/paste for look-alikes, compatibility, and availability (Gemini + Lens; Walmart multimodal plans).
  • Policy-driven value: Standing rules (brand, eco, delivery, returns) enforced by agents.
  • Post-purchase autopilot: Returns, warranty, and reorder cadence in chat (Klarna’s 11→2 minute resolutions).

The Universal Cart & “Chatbot Aggregators”

Assistants become channels with in-chat checkout (ACP/Instant Checkout). Ranking shifts from “blue links” to “best answer,” forcing merchants to expose structured, provable reasons-to-buy.

  • Single conversation spans discovery → comparison → payment.
  • Multi-item and multi-merchant carts emerging; assume agent-curated bundles.
  • Attribution evolves: assistant-referred revenue and selection share matter more than pageviews.

Module 4 · Maturity Model & 12-Month Build Plan

Retail AI Maturity (L1→L5)

  • L1 Content: AI-assisted PDP copy, images, translations (with guardrails).
  • L2 Service: AI support copilot + returns automation; track AHT/CSAT/containment.
  • L3 Conversational discovery: Embedded assistants (Rufus/Sparky-like), review synthesis, occasion shopping.
  • L4 Agent-ready data & APIs: Schema.org/GS1, real-time availability/pricing, explicit reasons-to-buy, post-purchase hooks.
  • L5 Agentic commerce: ACP participation, Instant Checkout, assistant channels treated like marketplaces with attribution & margin controls.

12-Month Plan (merchant)

0–90 days: Fill attribute gaps; add schema.org/Product; map SKUs to GS1 Digital Link; explain “why this pick”; launch service copilot for returns/status/refunds.
3–6 months: Apply for ChatGPT merchant onboarding; test Instant Checkout (single-item flows); build order/returns webhooks; publish spec sheets and comparison pages; track assistant selection/citation rate.
6–12 months: Expose occasion/bundle APIs; add policy endpoints (delivery windows, CO₂, price-match, return friction); build negotiation/risk checks with confirmation steps.

Module 5 · What to Prototype This Quarter

Experiments

  • “Describe & buy” concierge: Accept free-form goals; return shoppable bundles with swap/alternatives; test conversion vs. search-led flows.
  • Agent channel pilot: Wire a limited catalog to ACP/Instant Checkout; enforce strict price/stock guardrails; measure selection rate, margin, and drop-off.
  • Review synthesis with provenance: Surface why the model recommends items; cite review clusters/specs like Google/Amazon.

Risks & Mitigations

  • Disintermediation: Invest in distinctive reasons-to-buy, fast SLAs, identity standards so agents prefer you.
  • Data quality drift: Create attribute completeness KPIs; auto-validate feeds (price/stock/returns freshness).
  • Compliance & trust: Human-in-loop for high-risk categories; log agent decisions; expose recency and sources.
  • Voice/agent reversals: Instrument experiments; be ready to pivot (e.g., paused voice ordering pilots).

Bonus · Reverse Shopping & Negotiator Scenario

Reverse shopping: Users broadcast intent; merchant agents listen and bid with time-bound offers. User’s AI curates top options, shifting competition to “become the selected answer.”

Negotiator flow: AI assembles a multi-item basket, negotiates with merchant agents on price, and checks out once within budget—requiring dynamic, agent-accessible pricing rules and clear negotiation limits.

Glossary & Standards

  • ACP (Agentic Commerce Protocol): OpenAI/Stripe standard for agent ↔ merchant checkout flows.
  • Instant Checkout: In-chat purchase flow inside ChatGPT using ACP.
  • GS1 Digital Link: Canonical product identity URL tying GTIN/serials to trusted product data.
  • AIO (Assistant/Agent Optimization): Making your catalog selectable by assistants (structured data, provenance, reasons-to-buy).

TL;DR (strategy in one line)

Make your products legible to AI, make your operations callable by AI, and meet customers where their assistant lives.

Ready-to-Use Checklists

Product/Data Readiness

  • Complete schema.org/Product for top SKUs; validate in Search Console.
  • Publish GS1 Digital Link URLs; ensure SKU ↔ GTIN mapping.
  • Capture reasons-to-buy, pros/cons, and context tags (fit, climate, compatibility).
  • Expose availability, price, CO₂/ETA, and returns as APIs/webhooks for agents.

Experience & Ops

  • Design describe-and-delegate UX with swap/constraints support.
  • Instrument assistant-referred revenue, selection share, and citation rate.
  • Implement confirmation/negotiation guardrails and human-in-loop paths.
  • Track AHT/CSAT/containment for AI-first service; iterate policies.
